Content Policy

Last updated: June 2026

FaceMeet allows dating profiles and conversation features that support legitimate adult dating and matchmaking. FaceMeet does not host adult entertainment, explicit sexual media, paid private videos, paid live cam activity, escort listings, or user monetization.

Allowed content

Members may create a dating profile, record a current face-forward profile video, describe their interests, participate in private Spark Session video rooms, and message mutual matches in a respectful dating context.

Adult-content prohibition

FaceMeet prohibits nudity, sexually explicit content, pornographic content, sexual solicitation, escort services, prostitution, paid dating, compensation-based dating, OnlyFans-style promotion, adult entertainment, creator adult content, paid private videos, paid live cam activity, tipping, user-to-user payments, and user monetization.

Other prohibited content

  • CSAM, child sexual exploitation, grooming, trafficking, sexualization of minors, or any minor-safety violation.
  • Harassment, abuse, threats, hate, coercion, doxxing, or non-consensual intimate content.
  • Scams, spam, fake profiles, impersonation, misleading identity claims, or automated bot activity.
  • Illegal content, illegal transactions, or instructions to commit harm.
  • Solicitation for off-platform paid sexual services, paid companionship, compensated dating, paid private video, paid live cam, tips, gifts, or user-to-user payments.

Profile-video checks

FaceMeet uses profile-video moderation, automated sampled-frame video checks where configured, and manual admin review when needed. A profile video may be rejected, held for review, hidden from discovery, or require replacement if it violates this policy or cannot support a real dating profile.

Payments and content

FaceMeet payments are platform-controlled only. Members buy subscriptions, Spark credits, or Spark bundles from FaceMeet. Members cannot buy content from each other, sell private videos, request tips, pay for live cam activity, or monetize ordinary user profiles.
