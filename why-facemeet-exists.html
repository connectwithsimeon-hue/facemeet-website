Mission

Real connection should start face-to-face.

FaceMeet is a trusted network where meaningful connections begin through real conversations instead of profiles and messages. It helps real people meet through video-first presence, Live Topics, communities, events, and intentional face-to-face moments before private chat begins.

The Problem

Online connection is broken.

People are more visible than ever, but visibility is not the same as trust. FaceMeet exists because modern connection needs a better starting point.

1

Profile-first connection

Too many platforms ask people to judge each other through static profiles before any real conversation happens.

2

Followers, not connections

Social media made people visible, but not truly known or connected.

3

Cold networking

Professional networking became transactional, crowded, and disconnected from real trust.

4

Lower trust

Fake profiles, AI-generated identities, and catfishing make it harder to know who is real.

5

Low-intent chat

Messaging strangers without real context creates weak conversations and wasted attention.

The Belief

We believe trust should come before chat.

FaceMeet is designed around a simple belief: before people invest time, emotion, or attention, they should know there is a real person on the other side.

Video-first presence
FaceMeetings before private chat becomes the center
Live Topics for real-time conversations
Sparks to encourage intentional actions
Events and communities for real-world connection

Not profile-first guessing. Real moments.
