Mission

Real connection should start face-to-face.

The internet made it easier to meet people, but harder to know who is real. FaceMeet was built for a new kind of connection, one where real people meet through short video profiles, live conversations, and intentional face-to-face moments before private chat begins.

The Problem

Online connection is broken.

People are more visible than ever, but visibility is not the same as trust. FaceMeet exists because modern connection needs a better starting point.

1

Endless swiping

Dating apps turned people into swipe cards instead of real humans with voice, presence, and context.

2

Followers, not relationships

Social media made people visible, but not truly known or connected.

3

Cold networking

Professional networking became cold, transactional, and crowded with spammy messages.

4

Lower trust

Fake profiles, AI-generated identities, and catfishing make it harder to know who is real.

5

Low-intent chat

Messaging strangers without seeing them first creates weak conversations and wasted attention.

The Belief

We believe trust should come before chat.

FaceMeet is designed around a simple belief: before people invest time, emotion, or attention, they should know there is a real person on the other side.
