Why FaceMeet exists

Real connection should start face-to-face.

FaceMeet is a trusted network where meaningful connections begin through real conversations instead of profiles and messages — video-first presence, Live Topics, communities, events, and intentional face-to-face moments before private chat begins.

Online connection is broken

People are more visible than ever, but visibility is not the same as trust. Profile-first platforms ask people to judge each other through static profiles; social media made people visible but not truly known; networking became transactional; fake and AI-generated identities lowered trust everywhere.

We believe trust should come before chat

Before people invest time, emotion, or attention, they should know there is a real person on the other side: video-first presence, FaceMeetings before private chat becomes the center, Live Topics for real-time conversation, Sparks to encourage intentional actions, events and communities for real-world connection.

Not profile-first guessing. Real moments.

The old way: browse static profiles, guess from photos and bios, message before trust, wonder who is real. The FaceMeet way: see and hear real people, join Live Topics and communities, use Sparks for intentional actions, meet through real conversation.

One network for meaningful connection

Professional connections, founder networks, career opportunities, mentorship, communities, and events — one trusted network built around presence and intent.
