FaceMeet FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

Meaningful connections begin through real conversations. Here is how the pieces fit.

What is FaceMeet?

FaceMeet is a trusted network where meaningful connections begin through real conversations instead of profiles and messages. People use FaceMeet for Trusted Connections, Professional Connections, Founder Networks, Career Opportunities, Mentorship, Communities, Live Topics, and Events.

Is FaceMeet only one kind of connection?

No. FaceMeet is built for Trusted Connections, Professional Connections, Founder Networks, Career Opportunities, Mentorship, Communities, Live Topics, and Events.

What is a Follow?

A Follow helps you keep track of people and communities worth knowing while trust develops through real conversation.

What is a Spark?

A Spark is what you use to start a real conversation with someone. Because it costs a Spark, every conversation is one both people chose.

What is a FaceMeeting?

A FaceMeeting is a real face-to-face conversation that can grow from a Live Topic, community, professional introduction, mentorship path, event, or Spark.

What are Live Topics?

Live Topics are pop-up conversations with hosts, speakers, and viewers around a specific idea or shared intent.

Who is FaceMeet for?

FaceMeet is for people seeking Trusted Connections, Professional Connections, Founder Networks, Career Opportunities, Mentorship, Communities, Live Topics, and Events.

How does safety work?

FaceMeet uses video-first profiles, reporting, blocking, profile moderation, controlled Live Topic access, and clear community standards.

How do I get access?

Download FaceMeet on the App Store or Google Play, or open app.facemeet.app in your browser.
