FAQ

Questions about FaceMeet.

Everything you need to know about the video-first network for professional and meaningful connections.

What is FaceMeet?

FaceMeet is a video-first network for professional and meaningful connections. People use FaceMeet to follow interesting people, join Live Topics, and Spark real FaceMeetings.

Is FaceMeet only one kind of connection?

No. FaceMeet is built for professional connections, friendship, Social Connections, and Live Topic Conversations.

What is a Follow?

A Follow helps you keep up with people worth knowing without forcing an awkward connection request.

What is a Spark?

A Spark is a high-intent signal used when you want a real FaceMeeting with someone.

What is a FaceMeeting?

A FaceMeeting is a real face-to-face conversation that can grow from a profile, Follow, Live Topic, or Spark.

What are Live Topics?

Live Topics are pop-up conversations with hosts, speakers, and viewers around a specific idea or shared intent.

Who is FaceMeet for?

FaceMeet is for people seeking professional connections, founder/operator networks, career opportunities, mentorship, friendship, and meaningful conversations.

How does safety work?

FaceMeet uses video-first profiles, reporting, blocking, profile moderation, controlled Live Topic access, and clear community standards.

How do I get access?

Download FaceMeet on Google Play, open supported web access, or join the Founding Circle while early rooms are being built.
