Get FaceMeet

On desktop

Four steps, all of them in the browser.

You need a webcam and a microphone, and to allow the browser to use them when it asks.

01

Sign in

Continue with Google, continue with Apple, or your email and password. Use the same account as the app — if you already have one, that's the one.

02

Post the role

Role title, company, location, and a paragraph on what you need in your own words. That is the whole form.

03

Record the brief

The recorder opens right after you create the role. Sixty seconds with a countdown on screen, and you can re-record until you're happy with it.

04

It goes live after review

Your video is checked, then the role is live. Your roles list shows where each one stands.

On your phone

Four steps in the app.

01

Get the app

iPhone or Android. Sign up with your work email — it takes a minute and it's free.

02

Record your own profile

Thirty seconds saying who you are and who you hire for. Candidates watch this before they decide to answer you, so it matters.

03

Open Post a role

Title, one paragraph, remote, hybrid or onsite, sector, and a salary range. Then record a 60‑second pitch for the role — the thing you'd actually say across a table.

04

Publish

Your video goes through a quick check, then the role is live and we start bringing you people.

What to say on camera

Sixty seconds, not a job spec.

The recording is the reason someone stops scrolling and answers. The best ones sound like a person, not a posting.

Open with

Who you are

Your name, the company, and what it does in one sentence.

Then

The actual problem

What this person will be handed in their first month. Specific beats impressive.

Then

Who fits

The two or three things that genuinely matter — and say what doesn't, so the wrong people self-select out.

Close with

What happens next

Tell them you'll watch their answer yourself. That's why they'll bother.

Then what

The answers come back as a grid.

Open the role and you're looking at faces, best answers first. Every tile is a video you can play.

→

Thirty-second answers

Each person records a fresh answer to your brief. If someone hasn't, you see their profile video instead and the tile tells you so.

→

Read against your brief

Open one and you get the fit, the evidence behind it, what's missing, and a transcript of what they said.

→

Share a candidate

Copy a link and send it to a colleague. They don't need an account to watch it.

→

Book a call

Two Sparks books it and they're notified on their phone. The candidate is never charged.
