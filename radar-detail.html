What Radar is

The read that leads to a meeting.

Radar tracks grants, accelerators, investors, events and AI news — verified, summarised and served daily inside FaceMeet. Every item also shows the members going after it.

SUMMARISED

The real source, one tap away

Each item carries a plain summary and a link to where it came from, so you can check it yourself before you act on it.

DEADLINED

Pinned until it passes

Where there is a closing date, Radar counts it down for you — and takes the item off the feed once it has gone.

SHARED

See who else is on it

Mark an item interested or applying and your face joins it. Meet the other members going after the same thing, face to face.
