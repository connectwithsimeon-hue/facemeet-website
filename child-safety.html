FaceMeet Child Safety Standards

Last updated: May 2026

FaceMeet is an adults-only video-first connection network intended for users 18+. We are committed to helping keep minors safe and prohibiting any behavior, content, or activity that exploits, endangers, sexualizes, or abuses minors.

Our Standards

FaceMeet prohibits child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child sexual exploitation, grooming, sexualization of minors, trafficking, solicitation, or any attempt to exploit or endanger minors.

Users must not upload, request, share, transmit, promote, or create content involving minors in sexual, exploitative, abusive, or unsafe contexts.

FaceMeet provides in-app reporting and blocking tools for suspicious, abusive, or unsafe behavior. Reports involving child safety are reviewed and escalated with priority.

How to Report a Child Safety Concern

  • Report inside the FaceMeet app using the report or block tools.
  • Email support@facemeet.app.
  • Include the username, screenshots, date/time, and a description of the concern if safe to do so.

Our Enforcement Actions

  • Content removal.
  • Account suspension or ban.
  • Reporting suspected CSAM or child exploitation to appropriate regional, national, or international authorities where legally required.
  • Preservation of relevant evidence where legally required.

Cooperation With Authorities

FaceMeet may remove content, suspend or ban accounts, preserve relevant evidence, and report suspected CSAM or child exploitation to appropriate regional, national, or international authorities where legally required.

FaceMeet cooperates with law enforcement and child safety authorities when legally required.

Contact

For child safety concerns, contact us at support@facemeet.app.
