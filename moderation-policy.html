Moderation Policy

Last updated: June 2026

FaceMeet uses layered moderation to support a legitimate adult trusted-connection experience for Professional Connections, Founder Networks, Career Opportunities, Mentorship, Communities, Live Topics, and Events. We do not operate as an adult entertainment platform, webcam marketplace, escort marketplace, creator-payout platform, paid private-video platform, tipping platform, user monetization platform, or marketplace for compensated dating.

For underwriting clarity: FaceMeet is not an adult entertainment platform, not a webcam marketplace, not an escort marketplace, not a creator-payout platform, not a paid private-video platform, not a tipping platform, not a user monetization platform, and not a marketplace for compensated dating.

Moderation controls

  • Profile-video moderation before profiles become broadly eligible for discovery.
  • Automated sampled-frame video checks and face-presence checks where configured.
  • Manual admin review for videos or profiles marked pending, needs review, or rejected.
  • User reporting and user blocking from profile and chat surfaces.
  • Admin review queues for reports, profile-safety issues, blocked-user patterns, and profile videos.
  • Private tokenized Spark Session video rooms for mutual connection interactions.
  • Platform-controlled payments only for subscriptions, Spark credits, and Spark bundles.

What admins review

Admin reviewers may review reported users, reported behavior, profile video status, moderation reasons, uploaded profile-video URLs, profile completion status, and report details. Admin reviewers can mark reports reviewing, resolved, or dismissed, and can approve, reject, or hold profile videos for further review.

Enforcement outcomes

Depending on severity and available evidence, FaceMeet may remove content, reject a profile video, keep a profile out of discovery, require a new profile video, restrict access, terminate an account, preserve evidence where legally required, or report suspected CSAM or child exploitation to appropriate authorities where legally required.

Report and block instructions

Members can block or report another user from their profile or from chat. Reports should include the reason, relevant context, and screenshots if safe. Members may also contact support@facemeet.app.

Controls we do not claim

FaceMeet does not claim live AI moderation of Spark Sessions, government ID verification, or third-party age-verification provider integration on this website. Spark Sessions are private tokenized video rooms, and FaceMeet states in its Privacy Policy that video calls are not recorded by FaceMeet.

FaceMeet is a professional network where your profile is a 30‑second video instead of a résumé. Founders, investors, talent, mentors and recruiters find each other by what they actually said on camera — and for hiring, a recorded brief becomes a ranked shortlist of people worth meeting.
