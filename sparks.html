FAQ

Spark questions, answered.

Can I use FaceMeet without buying Sparks?

Yes. New members start with 3 starter Sparks, and the first 20 viewer seats in a Live Topic are free.

Do new members get free Sparks?

Yes. Every new member receives 3 starter Sparks once when they join.

Are there weekly free Spark refills?

No. Starter Sparks are granted once. FaceMeet does not offer weekly free Spark refills.

What is Spark+?

Spark+ is $14.99/month and gives members 2 daily bonus Sparks.

What is Gold?

Gold is $29.99/month and gives members 5 daily bonus Sparks.

What happens when the first 20 Live Topic viewer seats are full?

Additional viewers can join by using 1 Spark while access is available.

Can I buy Sparks anytime?

Yes. Members can buy Starter, Value, or Power Spark Packs whenever they need more Sparks.
