How it works

You record 60 seconds. We bring you 20 people worth meeting.

No job spec to write, no résumé pile to read, no weeks of screening. Say what you need out loud and we do the rest.

01

You post a video

Sixty seconds saying what you're looking for — the way you'd explain it to a colleague. That's the whole job post.

02

We go and find them

Our sourcing agent picks the right places for your role — FaceMeet's own network, GitHub for technical hires, licensed professional data for everyone else — and finds people who genuinely fit.

03

We invite them, with your video

They don't get a job ad. They get you, explaining the role yourself. That's why they answer.

04

They answer on camera

Thirty seconds saying why they're right for it, plus their résumé. Anyone who can't be bothered filters themselves out.

05

Our AI scores every answer

Each one is read against what you actually asked for — what they can evidence, and what they're missing. No black-box number.

06

You get the top 10–20

Only the ones who stand up to the brief. Watch them, then interview or pass. Most of it happens the same day.
