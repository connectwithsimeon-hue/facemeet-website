FaceMeet is a trusted network where meaningful connections begin through real conversations, professional introductions, communities, Live Topics, and events. FaceMeet is not an adult entertainment platform, not a webcam marketplace, not an escort marketplace, not a creator-payout platform, not a paid private-video platform, not a tipping platform, not a user monetization platform, and not a marketplace for compensated dating.

What users can buy

  • Subscriptions such as Spark+ or Gold, billed on a recurring basis until canceled.
  • Spark credits or Spark bundles, which are one-time purchases used for FaceMeet platform features.
  • Platform-controlled access to platform connection features operated by FaceMeet.

What users cannot buy or sell

FaceMeet does not allow user-to-user payments, tipping, paid private videos, paid live cam activity, paid messages, escort services, prostitution, paid dating, compensation-based dating, adult entertainment, creator adult content, OnlyFans-style promotion, or user monetization. In plain terms: no user-to-user payments, no tipping, and no paid private videos.

Subscriptions and cancellations

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled before the next billing date. Users can cancel in app settings or through the available billing provider or app store billing settings. Cancellation prevents future renewals but does not automatically refund past paid periods.

Spark credits and bundles

Spark credits and bundles are digital credits for platform-controlled connection features. Used digital credits are generally non-refundable. Unused credits remain available according to the applicable plan or promotion.

Creator and referral promotions

FaceMeet may run marketing referral or affiliate-style creator promotions. These promotions are for helping FaceMeet acquire verified members and are not adult creator monetization, user-to-user payments, tipping, paid private video, paid live cam activity, or ordinary user payouts.

Billing support

For billing questions or refund review, contact support@facemeet.app. See the Refund Policy for more detail.
