THE SHARE LOOP

Share a grant. Earn Sparks.

Every Radar item has a public page. Send it to a friend inside FaceMeet in one tap, or share it anywhere outside — and when someone joins FaceMeet through your link, you earn Sparks.

MEET ON IT

The feed shows who else is in.

Tap "Interested" or "I'm applying" and your face joins the card. See the other members on the same opportunity and Spark them — co-applicants, teammates, and rivals worth knowing.
