Pitch Rooms

Pitch live. Get seen.
Match with real interest.

Investors host live Pitch Rooms. Founders take the stage for timed pitches. The room watches, and mutual interest becomes a direct introduction — no cold emails, no decks into the void.

1
◈
Investors host

An investor opens a Pitch Room around their thesis — pre-seed fintech, B2B SaaS, whatever they back.

2
⚡
Founders take the stage

Founders apply with Sparks and pitch live in timed rounds — everyone in the room sees presence, not paperwork.

3
▣

When interest is mutual, FaceMeet opens the conversation directly. The pitch was the meeting.

get paid.
