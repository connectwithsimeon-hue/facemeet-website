Safety and conduct

Community Guidelines

Last updated: June 2026

FaceMeet is a video-first network for adults seeking professional and meaningful connections. It is not an adult entertainment platform, not a webcam marketplace, not an escort marketplace, not a creator-payout platform, not a paid private-video platform, not a tipping platform, not a user monetization platform, and not a marketplace for compensated dating.

What FaceMeet is for

FaceMeet helps adults meet through profile videos, mutual interest, and private tokenized Spark Session video rooms. Platform payments are controlled by FaceMeet and are limited to subscriptions, Spark credits, and Spark bundles. Users may not sell access to themselves, their content, their videos, or their time.

Respectful connection conduct

  • Use your real identity and a current live-recorded profile video.
  • Treat other members with respect before, during, and after Spark Sessions.
  • Accept rejection and boundaries without pressure, retaliation, harassment, or abuse.
  • Use report and block tools when another member behaves unsafely or violates these guidelines.

Prohibited conduct and content

FaceMeet does not allow nudity, sexually explicit content, pornographic content, sexual solicitation, escort services, prostitution, paid dating, compensation-based dating, OnlyFans-style promotion, adult entertainment, creator adult content, paid private videos, paid live cam activity, tipping, user-to-user payments, or user monetization.

FaceMeet also prohibits harassment, abuse, threats, hate, scams, spam, fake profiles, impersonation, illegal content, CSAM, child sexual exploitation, grooming, trafficking, or any attempt to sexualize, exploit, or endanger minors.

Reporting and blocking

Members can block or report a person from a profile or chat screen. Reports are reviewed through admin review queues. Blocks remove users from the blocker's feed and chat view. For urgent safety concerns, email support@facemeet.app.

Enforcement

FaceMeet may remove content, reject or hide profile videos, restrict visibility, close reports as resolved or dismissed, suspend access, terminate accounts, preserve evidence where legally required, and cooperate with appropriate authorities when legally required.
